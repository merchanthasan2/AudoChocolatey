Which motion style feels right?

These are live loops showing the same three moments with two personalities. Watch each for about 10 seconds. Search: results arrive. Install: the button turns into a download ring, then installs, then shows a tick. Update all: rows finish one by one and the counter ticks down.

1 · Crisp & quick

200 ms, no bounce. Like Windows 11 and Linear. It feels fast and gets out of the way.

2 · Springy & lively

About 400 ms with a soft overshoot. Like iOS and the App Store. It feels playful and spontaneous.

Either way, users who turn on "Reduce motion" in Windows get simple fades, and nothing waits on an animation, so real progress always drives the visuals.
